Ramadan 1448

8 February 2027 – 8 March 2027 · 29 days

163 days to Ramadan

The dates below follow the Umm al-Qurā calendar. The start of Ramadan may shift by a day where the month is begun on local moon sighting — follow your local masjid.

What Sirāj does during Ramadan

Suhoor and iftar reminders

Notifications before suhoor ends and at maghrib, computed on your device for wherever you are.

Fasting tracker

Mark each day kept, and any you owe. Your record stays on your device unless you choose to sync it.

Qur'an and taraweeh

The whole Qur'an with recitation and a reading plan, so a juz' a night is a plan rather than a hope.

The last ten nights

Adhkār and duʿāʾ for Laylat al-Qadr, and a Hijri calendar that knows which night you are on.

Eid al-Fitr

Expected on 9 March 2027, on the sighting of the Shawwāl crescent.
