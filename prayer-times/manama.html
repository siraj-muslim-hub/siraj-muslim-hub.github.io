How these times are calculated

Times are computed from the sun's position at 26.2285°, 50.5860° using Umm al-Qurā University, Makkah. ʿAsr uses the Shāfiʿī / Mālikī / Ḥanbalī shadow length. Daylight saving is applied automatically from the Asia/Bahrain timezone. Fajr is placed at 18.5° below the horizon and ʿIshāʾ 90 minutes after Maghrib.

Conventions differ, and a mosque near you may publish times a few minutes either side of these — follow your local masjid where they differ. In the app you can choose the method, the ʿAsr madhhab, and per-prayer manual adjustments so the times match your community exactly.
